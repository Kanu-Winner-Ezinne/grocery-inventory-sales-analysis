# Grocery Inventory & Sales Performance Analysis|Power BI

## Project Overview

This project explores grocery inventory and sales performance using Power BI to understand revenue contribution, inventory distribution, product demand patterns, warehouse efficiency, and supplier performance.

The analysis combines sales and inventory data to identify revenue-generating products, evaluate stock distribution, examine demand variability, and highlight opportunities for more efficient inventory management.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze revenue performance across products and product categories.
- Identify high-value products using ABC classification.
- Examine product demand patterns using XYZ classification.
- Compare sales volume and inventory value across warehouses.
- Evaluate supplier revenue contribution and inventory turnover.
- Identify potential inventory imbalances and opportunities for improved stock planning.

## Tools and Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Inventory and Sales Analysis

## Dashboard Overview

The Power BI report contains three analytical pages.

### 1. Product Performance Analysis

This page examines product revenue contribution, demand variability, and category performance.

Key analyses:
- ABC-XYZ Product Performance Matrix
- Revenue Distribution by Product Demand
- Revenue Contribution by ABC Category
- Product Performance Details
- Revenue Contribution by Product Category

Key Findings:
- Category A products generate approximately 275K in revenue, contributing around 80% of total revenue.
- Products with variable and uncertain demand contribute approximately 69% of revenue.
- Fruits & Vegetables, Beverages, and Seafood are the leading revenue-generating categories.
- Bakery and Oils & Fats contribute the lowest revenue among product categories.

### 2. Inventory and Demand Trends

This page explores historical sales and inventory movements alongside future sales and revenue forecasts.

Key analyses:
- Sales and Inventory Trends
- Top 10 Product Performance
- Sales Volume Forecast Trends
- Revenue Forecast

Key Findings:
- Arabica Coffee and White Tea are the leading revenue-generating products.
- Inventory value fluctuates considerably over time relative to sales activity.
- Forecasts indicate relatively stable future sales volume and revenue, without a strong sustained growth trend.

### 3. Operational Efficiency & Stock Analysis

This page evaluates warehouse inventory distribution, supplier performance, and inventory movement.

Key analyses:
- Inventory Value vs Sales Volume by Warehouse
- Top 10 Vendors by Revenue
- Inventory Composition by Warehouse
- Operational Performance Details
- Top 10 Suppliers by Inventory Turnover

Key Findings:
- Inventory value exceeds sales volume across all analyzed warehouses, suggesting potential inventory imbalances.
- Katz records the highest inventory turnover among the displayed suppliers, while Youfeed generates the highest revenue.
- Youfeed and Meeveo are the leading revenue-generating vendors.
- High inventory values relative to sales suggest substantial capital tied up in stock.
- Warehouses maintain similar inventory category compositions, including perishable products.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could support better inventory and sales management:

- Prioritize Category A products when planning replenishment and monitoring inventory availability.
- Strengthen demand forecasting for products with variable and uncertain demand.
- Review inventory levels across warehouses to identify potential overstocking and slow-moving stock.
- Monitor supplier revenue concentration and evaluate supplier diversification opportunities.
- Review inventory allocation for perishable products to support better stock rotation and reduce potential spoilage.
- Monitor supplier inventory turnover alongside revenue contribution to improve purchasing decisions.

## Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform inventory and sales data into actionable business insights. By combining product performance, demand analysis, inventory trends, warehouse comparison, and supplier evaluation, the dashboard supports more informed decisions around inventory planning, purchasing, and operational efficiency.
